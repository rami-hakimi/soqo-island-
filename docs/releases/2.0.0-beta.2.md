**Soqo Island 2.0.0-beta.2 — first public beta (pre-release)**

Soqo Island is a floating desktop companion for Windows 10 and 11: a small glass capsule at
the top of the screen that shows what matters right now (the song playing, a running timer, a
finished download, the next meeting) and opens on hover into a panel with music controls,
apps, files, downloads and system information. Soqi the Dolphin, an original character, lives
beside the island and reacts to what happens on your PC.

This is a **beta pre-release** for testing and feedback. It is not marked as the latest stable
release.

## Highlights
- **Soqi the Dolphin**: ten moods driven by real events (downloads, music, network, your
  return), a playful reaction when you click her, gentle idle motion. Switchable off; follows
  Windows' reduced-motion setting.
- **Media controls** for whatever Windows is playing, with a floating YouTube mini player.
- **Timers and focus sessions**, with a running-timer pill in Small, Medium or Large that can
  stay visible over full-screen apps.
- **Downloads, notifications, smart clipboard, app launcher, shelf, weather, CPU/RAM.**
- **English, Arabic and Dutch**, switchable live, with full right-to-left layouts for Arabic.
- **Optional Islamic Features** (prayer times calculated on your PC, notifications, dhikr,
  du'a and Qur'an verses): **off by default** for new installations, one switch under
  Settings › Features.
- **Privacy**: no account, no telemetry; clipboard history in memory only; the live-glass
  screen capture can be switched off and pauses by itself while the screen is locked or a
  screen recorder is running.
- **Installed-app behaviour**: quiet launch at sign-in if you want it, settings kept across
  upgrades, startup entry removed by the uninstaller.

## Installation
1. Download `Soqo-Island-Setup-2.0.0-beta.2.exe` from the assets below (optionally check it
   against `SHA256SUMS.txt`).
2. Run it. **The installer is not code-signed, so Microsoft SmartScreen may show "Windows
   protected your PC".** Only continue (*More info → Run anyway*) for a file downloaded from
   this Releases page.
3. Follow the installer (English, Arabic or Dutch; installs for the current user, no
   administrator rights). The island appears at the top of your screen; right-click it or the
   tray icon for Settings.

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Installing over an existing copy keeps your settings.

## Known beta limitations
- Unsigned installer (SmartScreen warning on first run).
- **The Arabic and Dutch translations still need a native-speaker review.**
- **Testing has been done primarily on one Windows 11 machine at 125 % display scaling**
  with a single monitor; Windows 10, multi-monitor and other scaling factors are untested.
- The adhan sound is a soft chime, not a recorded adhan.
- Auto-update is exercised for the first time with this release.
