# Soqo Island 2.0.0-beta.2 — release notes (draft, unpublished)

**A Living Island for Windows.** Soqo Island puts your music, downloads, apps, files and
alerts in one glass capsule at the top of the screen, with Soqi the Dolphin for company. This
is the first public beta of the Soqo Island rebuild, based on the open-source Nova Island
project (GPL-3.0). beta.1 was an internal build and was never published.

## Highlights
- **Soqi**, an original dolphin companion with ten moods that reacts to what actually
  happens, and now to you: click her for a short swim and a smile; at rest she floats, blinks
  and glances around; she sways to music.
- **English, Arabic and Dutch**, switchable live, with full right-to-left layouts for Arabic.
- **Optional Islamic features** (prayer times, reminders, Qur'an verses) under
  Settings › Features — off by default for new installations, one switch to turn on.
- **Timer pill in three sizes** (Settings › Timer) with pause/reset on hover and an option to
  stay visible over full-screen apps.
- **Privacy-first**: no accounts, no telemetry; clipboard history in memory only; a switch to
  turn the live glass backdrop (screen capture under the island) off entirely.
- **Hardened**: sandboxed renderers, strict content-security policies, Electron fuses, typed
  IPC validation.
- **Installed-app behaviour**: quiet launch at sign-in, startup kept across upgrades, removed
  by the uninstaller.
- **Fixes**: reminder and scene cards close on the first click of their × and release the
  mouse at once; the Start-menu catalogue no longer comes back empty on the first scan.

## Requirements
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

## Install
Download `Soqo-Island-Setup-2.0.0-beta.2.exe`. The build is **not code-signed**, so Windows
SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC"; only continue (*More info → Run anyway*)
for a file downloaded from this repository's Releases page. The installer offers English,
Arabic and Dutch. Installing over an existing copy keeps your settings.

## Coming from Nova Island
Your settings are imported once from `%APPDATA%\dynamic-nova`; the old folder is left as it
was. If your prayer features were at their defaults, Soqo Island asks once whether to keep
them.

## Known limitations
- Unsigned build (SmartScreen warning).
- Arabic and Dutch text await a second native-speaker review.
- The adhan sound is a soft chime.
- Tested on one Windows 11 machine at 125 % scaling with one monitor; Windows 10,
  multi-monitor and other scaling factors are untested.
